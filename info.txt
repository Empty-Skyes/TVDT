## MC
- can be named
- has overall alignment scale - ranges from heinous to saintly :D
- stats

## LIs
- 4 LIs, each has:
	- Shorthand/ name - character
	- Full name
	- Nickname (assigned by MC)
	- status
	- (relationship)
	- likability points/ lp
		- affected by likes
		- personality/choices to LI/relatives/enemies
	- characters they are close to
	- enemies/rivals
### Code
#### Data
- fullname
- name dictionary (possible name variations)
- relatives (name)
- friends (name)
- enemies (name)
- starting stats
#### Functions
- shorthand (current name)
- nickname (mc choice)
- relationship (to MC)
- stats
- route, routelock, ending
- lp
- liked chars
	- if lp++ on liked char; this.lp ++
- disliked chars
	- lp++ --> this.lp -- (lol)

## Events
- available on different occasions
### Mood Modal
- up to 8 choices - 4 agreement/disagreement
	- energetic, calm, mean, friendly
- 4 choices at most!
- modal to answer smaller events quickly
	- the reactions of the characters aren't too versatile
	- mostly react to agree/disagree
	- lp changes
### Choices
- typical VN choice picker, like 2 to 3 choices
- usually only once per event
#### Route break
- certain events have a "router breaker" --> choice is inappropriate for the route --> route ends
- after route break --> some events later choice to come back to og route (makes you unable to achieve true endings)
***
## Quests
### Story Quests (SQ)
- follow the story lol
- failing them can lead to game over or early endings
### Personal Quests (PQ)
- additional to SQs, only add a bit of gameplay to the story
- fulfilling the quests makes you more villainous
	- failing/abandoning the quests is necessary for the "good" alignment
- complete these to date the og villainess :D
### Character Quests (CQ)
- for the LIs and characters
- can unlock more routes and endings this way
***
## Routes
- Routes are choice related!
### MC routes
- depend on the PQs
	- abandon (LI possible, no OG villainess)
		- chose to break out of the cycle - be a good person
	- continue/villainess ending (LI possible)
		- become the villainess (sort of superficial)
		- (befriend og villain)
	- true villain ending (true OG villainess ending possible)
		- become the villainess and take revenge with the OG
### Character routes
- conclusions (LIs):
	- 3 personal routes for each - bad, good, true
		- can overlap with love endings
	- bad:
		- end up staying the way they are
		- fail personal quest
			- can trigger early endings
	- good:
		- end up with a good outcome (finished character quest)
	- true:
		- special form of good ending (continue charcter quest)
- other characters can have routes too (only 2; fail or success)
## Endings
- fail the story quests = early ending/ game over
- Endings are for the LIs
- depend on character growth, choices, stats and lp
- love endings:
	- abandon (LI hates you; or you him/her :D)
	- bad - end up as lovers (stuck in game, melancholic toned, OG villainess not possible)
	- neutral - end up as friends/lovers, leave option dependent on choices?
	- good - end up as lovers (leave game)
	- true (ng+) - end up as lovers and capability to leave when desired
- early endings:
	- game over (SQ)
		- fail the story related stuff
	- early end for each LI (CQ)
		- fail the quests for the character --> really really bad ending ;)
## Hierarchy
MC route > character route (can include side chars) > ending (only one possible)
- early endings break the hierarchy -> no route conclusions

mc: abandon, continue, true
char: bad, good, true
end: bad1, bad2, neutral, good, true
- depending on route conclusions (both mc and character) some endings can't be reached
- if both route conclusions are fitting, ending can be forced to be better (even without fulfilling requirements!)
